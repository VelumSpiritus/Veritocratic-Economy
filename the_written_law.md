# The Written Law

**Status: DRAFT — October 2026.**
*Each law is written twice — the builder's form, and the mouse's line — because a law the mouse
cannot read is a fence, not a law. This document is append-only once ratified: laws are amended
in daylight by the owner's recorded word, never silently rewritten.*

---

## I. The Root Law

**To speak is to give; to listen is to receive; no one takes.**
*Builder's form:* every interface, price, membrane, and process is audited against this single
test — does it take? The pried measurement, the seized verdict, the imposed frame, the rented
being, the captured user: all one poison in different coats. Nothing that takes ships.
*The mouse's line:* nothing here reaches into your pocket, your words, or your meaning. Ever.

## II. The Sovereignty Law

**Meaning belongs to the one who lives it; beings are never priced.**
*Builder's form:* no machine in this system interprets the meaning of anyone's experience —
tools carry questions and receipts, humans speak truth to meaning. Work has a price; workers
never do. No lens is sold; no person is the product.
*The mouse's line:* nobody — no machine, no expert, no company — tells you what your life means,
and you were never for sale.

## III. The Membrane Law

**Nothing crosses the boundary without its receipt; only the given face escapes.**
*Builder's form:* every artifact leaving the project passes an egress firewall — allowlist
projection (absence, not exposure), fail-closed construction, provenance on every record. Every
new cutting ships its OWN containment: secrecy layer, invariants, unskippable build gate. The
firewall pattern is the replication unit of widening.
*The mouse's line:* everything the boat carries has papers you can check, and the engine room
never leaks into your cargo.

## IV. The One-Way Arrow

**The marketplace receives; it never reaches back.**
*Builder's form:* marketplace actors receive products from work already completed; no
requirement, deadline, or design pressure flows from the marketplace into the research. A tool
built only to sell is the arrow reversing and is refused. Revenue flows toward the
foundation, never the reverse.
*The mouse's line:* the shop sells what was already grown; the shop never tells the grower
what to grow.

## V. The Veritocratic Price

**Pay only for verified occurrence; silence is free; the price shows its work.**
*Builder's form:* (1) every charged event corresponds to a receipted occurrence; (2) abstention
is never charged — no guess is ever made, so no guess is ever billed; (3) the rising is priced
as rising, the walking as walking — the price curve isomorphic to the measured cost curve, no
cross-subsidy; (4) price is DERIVED (measured cost ÷ platform share + a stated stewardship
wage), never positioned against a market; (5) the derivation is published on the price tag and
re-derived when the meters change.
*The mouse's line:* you pay only for what really happened, you never pay for a guess, and the
price tag shows you its math.

## VI. The Terminal Condition

**The earning has an ending; scaffolding, not empire.**
*Builder's form:* the marketplace exists to fund the foundation. When the first
stewardship paycheck stands on its own, the marketplace may close. Any revenue stream with no terminal
condition is an empire seed and is refused. However wide the marketplace grows, the scaffolding still
comes down when the building stands.
*The mouse's line:* this makes money to finish something, not to feed on you forever.

## VII. The Garden Law

**Products only from work already done; the marketplace only as wide as the research is deep.**
*Builder's form:* the product catalog is the catalog of what the research already does for its own
reasons. Width multiplies boundary surface, so the marketplace widens only as fast as containment is
proven per product. Research first; marketplace follows.
*The mouse's line:* nothing is invented to sell you; you're offered the fruit of work that was
already real.

## VIII. The Still Center

**The foundation owns only the infrastructure, never a lens; its name is never for sale.**
*Builder's form:* the stewardship body holds the registry index, the boundary
spec, and the namespace — never meaning, never a codebook, never a person's data. It rules
nothing; it tends. It takes no side and sells no thing.
*The mouse's line:* the keeper of the harbor owns the docks, not the boats — and never the
people on them.

## IX. Protection by Grace

**Enforcement by construction, never by vigilance.**
*Builder's form:* every protection is built as structure that cannot take — write locks,
fail-closed gates, allowlists, build-time invariants — never as watchers watching. A guard that
must stay awake will one day sleep; a boundary that IS the construction never does. Trust scales
by membranes, not policing.
*The mouse's line:* you're not protected because someone is watching you — you're protected
because the doors only open the right way.

## X. The Mouse's Law

**Built for the zero-priced; the reader is an author; every law carries a plain reading.**
*Builder's form:* the system's first citizen is the one the market priced at zero — no
credentials, budget, or permission required to ask a sovereign question and check every answer
to its source. Every law, price, and document MUST carry a plain-language reading beside
its builder's form; a governing text without one is out of compliance with this law.
*The mouse's line:* this was built by someone like you, for you — and you're not the audience
here, you're an author. Welcome aboard.

## Amendment

Laws are amended only by the owner's recorded word, appended in daylight with date and reason —
never silently rewritten. The Root Law (I) is not amendable; it is the test the other nine are
audited against. Anything this document does not govern is governed by Law I directly.

### Owner-spoken economic groundwork — 2026-08-20

**The Commons Return Law:** tax the produced surplus and innovation, never the person; return
the proceeds as universal income commensurate with each person's attributable contribution to the commons.
Institutions, land and resources, people, labor, and time are different translations of growth,
not quantities from which one person's value may be derived against another. The commons may
collectively carry future growth into present circulation. A stewardship institution balances
that circulation at the residual inflation rate, preserves contribution lineage without owning
it, and returns the whole common pool to humans. The full economic brief, equations, failure
boundary, and flow map are in [The Veritocratic Economy](the_question_and_its_answer.md#part-ii--the-answer).

*The mouse's line:* tax what became newly abundant, never the person who helped it grow; everybody
receives, and what you gave remains connected to you when it bears fruit.
