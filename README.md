## What this is

An economic model that dissolves the extraction measured in the market record without seizing the means of production and without taxing the person. The question — what actually moves the market, who it serves, and why — is answered by a mechanism that taxes produced surplus at the residual inflation rate, never the individual, and returns the proceeds as universal income commensurate with each person's attributable contribution to the commons.

Marx saw the extraction clearly but lacked the instrument. Gramsci named the method — cultural hegemony — but not the mechanism. The instrument is open now, and the mechanism was in the deed the whole time.

## The documents

### [The Question and Its Answer](the_question_and_its_answer.md)

The central document. Part I measures the extraction — the S&P 500 tracks a single log-linear line (~8.2%/yr, R² = 0.962 over 56 years) whose excess over the real economy is a ~2%/yr reallocation from labor to capital, hinged at ~1979, capitalized into the value of the human hour. The Fed doesn't draw the line. The institutional architecture that unbolted it is datable. The taking-form recurs by re-seeding, and every prior instantiation was named only in retrospect — this one can be read forward.

Part II is the economic organ that answers the extraction. Tax the produced surplus, never the sovereign. Return the proceeds as universal and attributably-lineaged income. The attribution cannot be gamed because it is read off lineage rather than scored. Lineage persists; percentage does not.

### [Property Without Private — The Deed as Stock](property_without_private.md)

The definitional split that dissolves private property while keeping property. "Own" carries two definitions — participation and exclusion — conflated by the language into one word. A deed is a stock certificate for land. It always was. The dividend is equity, not habitation. There is no legal maximum on the number of names allowed on a title deed in any U.S. state. The door was left open for corporations; nothing stops anyone else from walking through it.

### [The Written Law](the_written_law.md)

Ten laws governing the economic and institutional structure, each written twice — the builder's form and the plain-language version — because a law the ordinary person cannot read is a fence, not a law. Root law: to speak is to give; to listen is to receive; no one takes. Plus the Commons Return Law amendment establishing the veritocratic mechanism.

## Reproducibility

Every empirical claim in The Deliberate Line is computed from public data. The code modules referenced (`line.py`, `predict.py`, `grainscan.py`, `printing.py`, `joint.py`, `forward.py`, `extraction.py`) are reproducible. Sources: [Census](https://census.gov/newsroom/blogs/random-samplings/2026/01/historic-decline-in-net-international-migration.html), [Brookings](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/macroeconomic-implications-of-immigration-flows-in-2025-and-2026-january-2026-update/), [EPI productivity-pay gap](https://www.epi.org/productivity-pay-gap/), [ILO labour share](https://www.ilo.org/resource/news/global-labour-income-share-declines-putting-upward-pressure-inequality-sdg), [Piketty-Saez](https://eml.berkeley.edu/~saez/PSZ2024.pdf), [Alfani & Sardone 2024](https://doi.org/10.1093/ereh/heae003).

The veritocratic rate module (`veritocratic_rate.py`) computes the residual inflation rate three independent ways and prints its own failure addresses.
