# Property Without Private — The Deed as Stock

**Noah Davidson, October 2026**

---

## 1. The Definitional Split

The word "own" carries two definitions that never get separated:

1. **Own as participation** — to hold a share. Stock. Both names on the deed, both hold equal right. Adding you doesn't subtract me.
2. **Own as exclusion** — to possess exclusively. Dominion. Claim. Mine, not yours. Adding you takes from me.

The language doesn't distinguish them. "I own my house" fires both definitions simultaneously — participation AND exclusion — and the listener can't feel the seam.

The entire system of private property is built on the conflation of these two definitions. Every recoil against shared ownership comes from definition 2 firing when definition 1 is what's actually happening.

---

## 2. The Reorder — Stock First

Everyone understands stock. Start there.

**"If I add you to my Apple shares, did you take my phone?"**

The absurdity of the claim definition is immediately visible when the object is a stock, because nobody confuses holding shares of Apple with possessing Apple's products. The participation definition is the only one that survives.

Then: **"A deed is a stock certificate for land. It always was. Adding a name adds a shareholder. The house doesn't move."**

The differential lands because you gave them the clean definition first — through an object where the confusion can't happen — and THEN applied it to the one where it always does.

---

## 3. The Dividend Problem

With Apple stock the dividend is cash — abstract, divisible, clean. With a house the dividend is *living in it*. And living in it feels indivisible. The mind says: if they hold shares, they can show up.

But that's the dominion definition sneaking back in through the dividend. "Show up" is a claim. The actual dividend of a house is **equity** — which is just money again. Same as the stock. The house pays out when it sells, not when someone walks through the door.

The right to inhabit isn't the dividend. It's a separate agreement between the people on the title — the lease, the arrangement, the relation.

**A shareholder of Apple can't walk into the factory.**

**A name on a deed doesn't give someone a key. It gives them equity. Those are two different instruments.**

---

## 4. The Land Doesn't Hold Value

The institutional recoil protects a fiction: that the land itself holds dividend value that can be taken. It doesn't.

The government coupled land value to economic instruments — mortgages, tax assessments, zoning, lending ratios — and called the appreciation a "return." But the dirt didn't do anything. The coupling did.

The value is in the coupling, and the coupling is just a ledger entry that the government maintains. The "return on property" is inflationary accrual generated by the system, not by the land.

---

## 5. The Deed as Stock Certificate with Dividend Stipulation

A dividend displacement stipulated to a title divvies the inflationary accrual to each named person on the deed.

- The inflationary accrual that the government's coupling generates gets distributed to each name automatically.
- Nobody takes from anybody because nobody put it there — the system generated it.
- You're just routing what the coupling already produces to the people standing on it instead of to the bank holding the claim against it.

The bank's mortgage captures that accrual right now. A deed with thirty names and a dividend stipulation captures it instead for the people who actually live on the land.

This is not a new instrument. This is a co-op — but a co-op with no corporation in the middle. Just names on a deed and a stipulation for how the coupling's output gets split.

---

## 6. Entry and Exit — Two Sides of One Right

The choice to name yourself to a deed and the choice to leave with your accrued share are the same right from two sides. Entry and exit.

- **Entry:** Self-naming to the deed. A right for everyone.
- **Exit:** Leaving freely with what accrued under your name. A right for everyone.

If you can't leave freely with what accrued under your name, the deed is a trap — and that's just the mortgage by another name.

**No lock-in.** The share accrues while you're named. You leave, your portion follows you, the remaining names rebalance. Same as selling stock. Nobody's permission needed because nothing is being taken — you're withdrawing what the coupling generated under your name during your time.

The institution will say: what if everyone leaves at once. Same thing they say about a bank run. And the answer is the same: **if the structure only works because people can't leave, it was never their value to begin with.**

---

## 7. Private Dissolves — Property Stays

The right to property stays. The right to *private* property is what dissolves — because private was never about the property. Private was about the lock-in. The exclusion. The claim that your name means everyone else's name can't be there.

Remove the exclusion, keep the ownership. Every name accrues, every name can leave with what accrued. The property doesn't change. The privacy does — because privacy was the wall that made the extraction work.

**The argument isn't against property. It's against the lock.**

No one loses house or money or claim. Anyone can leave house with money and claim for money. Everyone is still better off than before.

---

## 8. The Housing Crisis Solved

There will be an empty house with no name on it. Open for someone else to claim and give name to deed.

Empty houses aren't a shortage — they're deeds with no names on them. Right now they sit empty because the lock holds them as assets for someone who isn't there. Remove the lock, the house opens when the last name leaves.

No construction needed. No policy. No subsidy. Just names flowing onto deeds and off of deeds with their accrual, and empty houses filling because there's nothing keeping them empty anymore.

**The waiting list becomes a deed list.**

---

## 9. The Legal Fact

There is no legal maximum on the number of names allowed on a title deed in any U.S. state. The door was left open for corporations, trusts, and LLCs to scale ownership without limit. Nothing stops anyone else from walking through it.

The deed predates the corporation. If you cap the number of names, you cap the size of the ownership — and the whole system runs on the ability to scale ownership without limit. They left the door open for themselves.

---

## 10. The Intellectual Lineage — Marx, Gramsci, and the Open Door

**Marx** saw the extraction clearly. Private property concentrates wealth through exclusion — the owner extracts, the non-owner labors, and the arrangement reproduces itself. His diagnosis stands. But his prescription was revolution — seize the means of production — because the instrument he needed didn't exist yet. The stock market in Marx's time was a private club. Shared investment wasn't available to the ordinary person. You couldn't buy into the means; you could only take them. So abolition had to come from the outside, by force, because there was no door to walk through from the inside.

The stock market is open now. Anyone can put a dollar in. The shared-ownership instrument Marx didn't have is the one everyone already uses — and the deed was always one.

**Gramsci** named the method. Cultural hegemony — the way the ruling arrangement maintains itself not through force but through every institution teaching the arrangement as natural. "Of course property is private. Of course a deed is a claim. Of course adding a name is a taking." That's not law. That's culture. The institution doesn't need to enforce the lock when everyone already believes it's how houses work.

Gramsci's answer was the war of position: don't storm the institution, transform it from within, using the institution's own logic and language. Not a frontal assault — a dissolution from inside.

This argument is that dissolution. It speaks the institution's own language — stock, equity, dividend, deed, title — and shows the institution's own instrument already contains the answer. The deed has no name limit. It was always a stock certificate. The dividend was always the coupling's output, not the land's. Entry and exit were always the same right. Nothing here is imported. The door was always open. They just taught everyone it was a wall.

Marx had the goal but not the instrument. Gramsci had the strategy but not the mechanism. The instrument is open now, and the mechanism was in the deed the whole time.

---

## 11. Connection — The Choice

The finding underneath it all: humans cooperate constantly, in conditions engineered for friction. Every day, on roads where a slight turn of the wheel could kill, people choose not to. By all institutional models built on competition as the natural state, they shouldn't be able to. But they do. Every time.

**The same species that chooses right on the road can share a deed.**
